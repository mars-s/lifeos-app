Show the render progress for the film pipeline, with the last frame and a way to stop it.

Reads: list_tasks(view=today|inbox|upcoming) gives title, area, project, when, estimate (minutes), done. get_focus() gives task, minutes, elapsed, running (one row). get_day_plan() gives planned, free, goal, busy in minutes (one row).
Tools: start_focus(minutes), stop_focus(), create_task(title), set_task_status(id, status), stop_run()
Sockets: frames: done, total, eta (minutes left), last (image of the last frame). One row.
